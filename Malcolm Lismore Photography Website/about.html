    • The First Steps

      Malcolm upgraded to a more advanced camera, immersing himself in the technical aspects of capturing the perfect shot. Experimenting with composition, lighting, and various camera settings.

      A turning point for Malcolm was when he came face to face with majestic creatures in their natural habitat. The challenge of capturing the fleeting moment ignited a new passion in him

      Now, as a seasoned professional photographer, Malcolm Lismore continues to find innspiration, forming stories through his photography. He shares his knowledge and experiences through workshops, aiming to inspire others to appreciate the beauty of the world and embrace their own creative journeys.
